Our Story & Purpose

Hadassah Hope Foundation was born out of a deep passion to see underprivileged Nigerians live with dignity, hope, and opportunity. Guided by our motto—“Let’s join hands in helping and bring back hope in souls”—we mobilize volunteers, donors, and community leaders to reach grassroots communities across Nigeria.

Whether through school supply distributions, menstrual hygiene awareness, community feeding, or orphanage visits, our focus remains clear: Be a reason someone smiles!

🎯 Our Mission

To empower vulnerable children, young, women, and families across Nigeria through sustainable support, health & hygiene initiatives, nutritional aid, and compassionate community outreach.

👁️ Our Vision

A compassionate Nigeria where every child has access to care, and every forgotten soul experiences restored hope and human dignity.
